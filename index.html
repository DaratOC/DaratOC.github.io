Phishing Awareness

That was a Phishing Link

That email you just interacted with was part of a phishing awareness exersize.

Don't be embarrassed, phishing attacks are constantly changing and designed to trick people. Please take a moment to review the red flags of this email to better prepare for real threats.

01

Red Flags

Here are all the Red Flags of this particular phishing email. I made this one to roughly simulate the attack we faced earlier this year, with an account being compromised and used to send more phishing emails.

      Context

      This "invite" came without notice or explaination. An unexplained invite with no context should be treated with suspicion. Even if this email came from our domain.

      Link

      If you hover over the link, you see that it leads to a github page rather than any expected invite location. At OC, we typically use Zoom or Google meet. This doesn't mean any link to these domains should be trusted, but it's another meaningful sign.

      Vague Invite

      Nothing in the email states what this invite is for.

02

What you should do next time

Please do the following if an email seems suspicious

      Reach out to Darrian

      If you are ever unsure of an email, please contact Darrian. He can safely scan any outgoing links or attatchments without opening them.

      Confirm with the individual directly

      In cases like this, if possible, it wouldn't hurt to reach out to the individual directly through another method of communication. You can give them a text/call to confirm that this is a legitamate invite.

      Report as Phishing

      If you are confident you have a phishing email, you can report it as phishing to pull this email from your inbox, help improve Google's filters, and alert Darrian so he can improve OC's filters.
